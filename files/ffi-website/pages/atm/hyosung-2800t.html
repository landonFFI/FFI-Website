The 2800T is the machine for a location that wants an ATM built into the wall, not sitting on the floor — weatherized, high-capacity, and designed for the kind of service ease that keeps a fixed installation running for years.

Features

  • 12.1" display with capacitive touch function keys
  • Supports up to three removable cassettes — up to 6,000-note total capacity
  • Weatherized, water-resistant exterior for outdoor and drive-up installs
  • Illuminated topper and vault service light for easier loading and maintenance
  • Braille keypad, digital receipts, and support for six languages
  • UL291 business-hours safe, with a Level 1 safe upgrade available

Why the 2800T?

This is the through-the-wall option on this page. If your location has floor space and doesn't need a permanent wall install, the Force covers the same high-capacity need at a lower price point. Choose the 2800T specifically when the installation itself needs to be through-the-wall.
