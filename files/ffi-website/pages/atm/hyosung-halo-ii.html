The Hyosung Halo II isn't the flashiest machine on this page — it's the one that just works, which is exactly why it's the best-selling retail ATM in the industry. If you're buying your first machine, or your fifth, the Halo II is the safe, proven choice.

Features

  • 10.1" high-resolution color display
  • EMV chip reader standard; NFC contactless reader available as a factory add-on
  • UL 291 business-hours safe with added break-in protection
  • PCI 3.0 compliant keypad, TLS-encrypted communications
  • Cassette options from 1,000 notes up to dual 2,000-note capacity
  • ADA compliant

Why the Halo II?

If you're weighing this against the Force or the 2800T, the difference is simple: the Force steps up in screen size and presence, and the 2800T steps up in cash capacity and adds a through-the-wall install, both at a higher price. The Halo II is the right call when reliability and value matter more than square footage of screen.
