## Free ATM placement
- FFI owns the machine and the cash; delivers, installs, loads, monitors remotely 24/7, repairs, and keeps machines EMV, ADA, and PCI compliant. The business provides floor space and a standard outlet.
- Placement: "as fast as 24 hours; sometimes up to two weeks, depending on scheduling."
- Host payment: share deposited by ACH at the start of each month, straight from the payment networks, plus a monthly email statement showing transaction count and earnings.
- Splits vary by location, volume, and history. NEVER publish a split, range, or typical share. Examples with a hypothetical number are allowed only if labeled "not a quote" (e.g., "say your share were $1.00, just to keep the math easy").
- Agreement: ALWAYS "a simple, standard placement agreement," framed as a benefit to the customer: it protects them by putting everything we promise in writing (what we handle, how and when they're paid, how long it runs, who is responsible if something goes wrong). NEVER say "no contract(s)" or "no long-term contract." Don't state agreement lengths.
- Theft: "The machine and the cash are ours, so if they're stolen, that loss falls on us, not you. Damage to your building is normally handled by your own property insurance. Our placement agreement puts this in writing."
- New ATM available to qualifying businesses with old, unreliable ATMs.

## Partnership program
- FFI owns, installs, maintains, and monitors the machine; the business loads the cash and gets 50% of the surcharge.

## Buying an ATM from FFI
- Prices: ALWAYS read current prices from `files/ffi-website/assets/data/catalog.json`. Never state a price from memory. Say "starting at" and "as of [month year]."
- Free shipping in the continental US only.
- Processing setup: free with FFI processing (PAI and DNS). Outside processor setup: $500.
- Programming: free programming sheet (self-program); factory programming $150; on-site technician $250.
- Warranty: 2-year manufacturer warranty on new machines; 90-day warranty on used.
- 24/7 support. Order placed within 12 hours of payment; delivery usually about two weeks; delays come from the manufacturer.
- Buyers keep 100% of the surcharge.
- Startup guide (emailed with the order): target location types, a sales guide for cold calls and walk-ins, and marketing materials. FFI gives its honest opinion on locations the buyer is considering. FFI does NOT find or sign locations, does NOT make sales calls, and does NOT promise income.

## Processing and services
- Free ATM processing for ATM owners through PAI and DNS, with 24/7 technical support through DPL Wireless and free over-the-phone reprogramming. Most machines can switch without an on-site visit.
- Settlement usually within 24 hours of the transaction on business days (Mon–Fri); weekends and bank holidays settle the next business day; some banks allow Saturday deposits, but it isn't typical.
- Cash loading service: $1 per transaction.
- Wireless ATM service billed monthly per ATM (don't state the price).
- FFI buys ATMs and routes: quick decisions, cash, any number of machines.
- Outdoor ATMs: FFI handles permits and proper permissions when needed.
- Events: pricing depends on distance, number of days, attendance, and how cash-heavy the event is (cash entry, cash bar, cash-only vendors, no card acceptance). Events cost more than long-term placements (more cash, on-site security, machines not bolted down). Pricing can include a setup fee, a revenue share, or both. Never publish event prices.
