The Onyx W solves one specific problem: not enough floor space for a standard ATM. Wall-mounted or countertop, it fits where a freestanding cabinet can't.

Features

  • Installs as wall-mount or countertop
  • 10.1" LED screen with light-up touch function keys
  • 1,000 or 2,000-note cassette options
  • Compact design built specifically for space-limited locations

Why the Onyx W?

This is the space-constrained answer, not a general-purpose pick. If you have normal floor space available, the G2500 or Onyx give you more configuration options for a similar price.
